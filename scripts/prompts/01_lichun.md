# 節氣：立春 (Lichun)

# 美學主題：破曉的生機 (The First Breath of Life)

## 🎨 藝術指導 (Artistic Direction)

畫面應捕捉「靜謐中爆發的生命力」。重點在於一支如黑炭般質感的枯枝（殘雪剛褪去），上面冒出一顆鮮嫩到發亮的翠綠芽點。

## 💡 核心提示詞 (Prompt)

A soulful, high-end nature photograph of 'Lichun'. An asymmetrical composition of a single, naturally weathered dark charcoal branch stretching elegantly from the side of the frame. One tiny, luminous emerald green bud is beginning to swell, catching a solitary shaft of golden winter-meeting-spring light. The background is an ethereal, painterly blur of cold mists and soft silver-grey shadows. Authentic ink-wash photography style with deep 'Liu-bai' (intentional empty space). Extreme sharpness on the wood texture and the translucent bud skin. Hasselblad X2D quality, zen aesthetic, tranquil and profound.

## 🚫 排除項 (Negative Prompt)

Symmetrical, centered composition, digital render, plastic texture, text markers, artificial writing, multiple crossing branches, oversaturated colors, flat lighting, busy background, human, 3d artifacts.
