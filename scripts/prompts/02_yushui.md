# 節氣：雨水 (Yushui)

# 美學主題：滋潤的詩意 (The Glistening Nourishment)

## 🎨 藝術指導 (Artistic Direction)

重點在於「水感」。不是大雨，而是空氣中飽和的水汽凝結成巨大、晶瑩剔透的雨滴，懸掛在翠綠的竹葉邊緣，即將墜落的一瞬間。

## 💡 核心提示詞 (Prompt)

Exquisite cinematic macro photography of 'Yushui'. A single, elegant bamboo leaf with a deep forest green hue, glistening with several massive, crystal-clear raindrops. The water droplets act as natural magnifying glasses, reflecting a soft misty landscape within. Misty, diffused silver and blue background. Cinematic soft lighting, hyper-detailed water textures, refreshing atmosphere. 8k resolution, professional nature photography aesthetic.

## 🚫 排除項 (Negative Prompt)

Dry leaves, brown spots, harsh sunlight, noisy background, abstract art, painting, multiple plants, cluttered frame, low resolution.
