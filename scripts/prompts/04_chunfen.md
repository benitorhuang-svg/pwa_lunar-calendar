# 節氣：春分 (Chunfen)
# 美學主題：極致平衡與純淨 (The Sublime Balance)

## 🎨 藝術指導 (Artistic Direction)
要勝過 NotebookLM 的版本，我們必須在「光影深度」與「花瓣質感」上做文章。
春分代表陰陽平衡，畫面應呈現完美的視覺對稱。背景不再是普通的藍天，而是一種帶有「高級感」的深邃綠松石色（Turquoise）或青瓷色（Celadon），並利用極致的逆光（Backlit）讓白玉蘭的花瓣呈現半透明的「玉質光感」。

## 💡 核心提示詞 (Prompt)
A breathtaking masterpiece of 'Chunfen'. Symmetrical composition featuring white magnolia flowers in full bloom against a deep, vibrant, and crystal-clear celadon-blue sky. High-end cinematic photography. The petals are backlit by a soft, warm spring sun, making them look semi-translucent like fine white jade. Sharpest macro focus on the delicate veins of the petals. Extreme contrast between the pure white blossoms and the deep saturated sky. Intentional negative space at the bottom to evoke a sense of flight and freedom. 8k resolution, Hasselblad clarity, ethereal lighting, monumental serenity.

## 🚫 排除項 (Negative Prompt)
Purple tints, blurry petals, multiple branches, messy composition, low contrast, washed out colors, gray sky, digital art artifacts, human elements, text markers.
