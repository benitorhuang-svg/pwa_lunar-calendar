# 節氣：驚蟄 (Jingzhe)

# 美學主題：雷鳴與覺醒 (The Great Awakening)

## 🎨 藝術指導 (Artistic Direction)

畫面應展現「泥土的呼吸」。深色的濕潤土壤中，一顆強而有力的嫩芽破土而出，周圍還有未融化的冰晶，象徵冬去春來的關鍵轉折。

## 💡 核心提示詞 (Prompt)

Cinematic low-angle macro photography of 'Jingzhe'. A powerful, bright lime-green sprout forcefully pushing through rich, dark, textured moist earth. Delicate, sharp ice crystals scattered around the base, beginning to melt. Warm, glowing golden-hour bokeh in the background, simulating the energy of hidden thunder. Sharpest focus on the sprout's veins, earthy textures, cinematic atmosphere, 8k resolution.

## 🚫 排除項 (Negative Prompt)

Flowers, tall grass, dry soil, flat light, noon sun, artificial garden, insects, cartoonish, oversaturated, low contrast.
